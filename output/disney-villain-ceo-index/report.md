# Disney Villain CEO Index
> Your company's next board seat might be best filled by the god of the dead — and the data backs it up.

## Opening

The corporate leadership industrial complex has spent decades writing books about what makes a great CEO. Strategic vision. Resource management. Crisis composure. The ability to read a room and bend it to your will. Emotional intelligence deployed as a precision instrument, not a hammer. And then, buried in the small print of every leadership framework, something consultants call "optimal decisiveness" — being tough enough to make the hard call without being so bloodthirsty you end up on the front page of the Journal for the wrong reasons.

Disney and Pixar villains, inadvertently, are a perfect stress-test laboratory for all seven of those traits. They run organizations. They manage (and mismanage) resources. They respond to setbacks in real time, on camera, with their full emotional range on display. And they fail — or succeed — for exactly the reasons that real executives do.

So we built a formula. Seven metrics. Thirty villains. One uncomfortable conclusion: **the candidates you'd most expect to ace the boardroom are not the ones the data picks.** Maleficent is mid-tier. Gaston is a catastrophe with good cheekbones. And the god of the dead, who runs a bureaucratic afterlife with finite soul-supply and impossible KPIs, scores 86.9 out of 100 — a number that beats every Disney villain in the room.

## The Surprise

**Maleficent is #17.** Not #1. Not top 5. Not even top 10. The Mistress of All Evil, the villain who has headlined Halloween for 65 years, ranks 17th out of 30 in this index with a score of 51.8 — squarely in C-tier, the "Needs Coaching" band. Here is why: she cursed an infant over a party invitation she didn't receive. This is a 2/10 Crisis Management score and a 2/10 Emotional Intelligence score. Her raw ruthlessness (10/10) pushes her RI_adj down to 6. A CEO who retaliates at the scale of "infant death curse" over a scheduling slight is a PR catastrophe waiting to happen. Maleficent fans will riot. The formula does not care.

**AUTO (#13, 57.0) is the most revealing data point in the dataset.** The autopilot from WALL-E scores 8/10 on both Resource Management and Crisis Management — tied for the highest Crisis Management score in the entire index, alongside Hades and Dr. Facilier. He has zero Charisma (1/10) and zero Emotional Intelligence (1/10). He is the perfect middle manager: efficient, consistent, completely incapable of handling ambiguity or human relationships. He is, in other words, many people's actual boss.

**Scar (#7, 66.8) is proof that strategic patience without emotional resilience is a glass cannon.** He scores 9/10 on Strategic Vision — tied highest with Syndrome — and 8/10 on Charisma. But his Crisis Management (4/10) collapses the moment things go sideways. Years of patient scheming undone by ego when confronted at Pride Rock. The formula specifically penalizes this: it's not enough to have a plan. You have to hold it together when the plan meets reality.

## Methodology

- **Index name**: Disney Villain CEO Index
- **Data sources**: Qualitative analysis of each villain's canonical Disney/Pixar film appearances (1937–2024). All scores assigned by rubric analysis — no external data collection required. Films available via Disney+.
- **Collection date**: March 2026
- **Sample size**: 30 villains
- **Scoring formula**:

```
CEO Score = (SV × 0.25) + (RM × 0.20) + (CP × 0.15) + (CM × 0.15) + (IA × 0.12) + (EI × 0.08) + (RI_adj × 0.05)
```

Where all raw scores are 0–10, and **RI_adj** (Ruthlessness Index, adjusted) = `10 − |raw_ruthlessness − 6|`. A raw ruthlessness score of 6 is CEO-optimal (decisive but not PR-toxic); extremes in either direction are penalized.

| Metric | Weight | Rationale |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Strategic Vision (SV) | 25% | Long-term planning, multi-step schemes, patience |
| Resource Management (RM) | 20% | Minion count, assets, infrastructure management |
| Charisma & Persuasion (CP) | 15% | Rallying followers, negotiating, manipulation |
| Crisis Management (CM) | 15% | Composure and pivot speed when plans fail |
| Innovation & Adaptability (IA) | 12% | Creative problem-solving, tech/magic leverage |
| Emotional Intelligence (EI) | 8% | Reading others, strategic empathy |
| Ruthlessness Index (RI_adj) | 5% | Optimal band: raw score 6 = 10 adj; extremes penalized |

## The Surprise

**Hinge is more manipulative than Match.com.** Match Group's flagship brand — the one that invented the "designed to be deleted" concept — scores higher on the Dark Pattern Index than the company's legacy product. Hinge's paywall manipulation is more sophisticated, more emotionally targeted, and more effective than Match.com's blunt-instrument notification spam. Match.com lies to you loudly. Hinge lies to you softly, at the exact moment you're most emotionally vulnerable.

**The feminist dating app has the highest FOMO score in the dataset.** Bumble's 24-hour match timer — sold to users as a power move that puts women in control — scores 10/10 on FOMO mechanics, tied with Thursday (which at least admits the time limit is the point). The timer creates urgency that benefits Bumble, not users. When matches expire, you can pay for Bumble Boost to extend them. The "empowerment" costs $29.99 a month.

**Grindr is not in this index for the reasons you think.** It scores 58.8 overall — mid-table. Its swipe addiction and paywall manipulation scores are modest. But it holds a 10/10 on data harvesting alone: 1,024 trackers documented by Mozilla Foundation, a €6.12M GDPR fine in Norway, HIV status shared with advertising partners, and location data sold in granularity precise enough for a Catholic media organization to run a surveillance operation on gay clergy. Grindr doesn't want to manipulate your emotions. It wants to sell your identity.

**Match Group is not a dating company. It's a manipulation infrastructure company.** Seven of the top twelve most manipulative apps in this index share a parent company: Tinder, Hinge, Match.com, OkCupid, Plenty of Fish, The League, and Pairs. Match Group doesn't own a portfolio of dating apps. It owns a portfolio of dark patterns deployed across different demographics.

## Methodology

- **Index name**: Dating App Dark Pattern Index
- **Data sources**:
  - FTC v. Match Group settlement documents (FTC.gov) — Match.com fake profiles, $14M settlement
  - Norwegian Consumer Council reports — Grindr GDPR violations, OkCupid A/B testing
  - Mozilla Foundation "Privacy Not Included" — Grindr (1,024 trackers, HIV data), Hinge (biometrics), Tantan (2019 citation)
  - NPR, CBS News, SF Standard — 2024 Match Group addiction lawsuit coverage
  - Appscrip, TextGod, multiple UX analyses — Tinder Elo/visibility throttling documentation
  - StartupSpells UX teardown — Tinder blur-to-reveal paywall mechanics
  - Bumble support docs / DatingApps.com — Bumble Boost pricing, timer mechanics
  - Fortbridge security research (2024) — Feeld vulnerabilities
  - FTC "76% of apps use dark patterns" study (2024) — via TechCrunch, FTC.gov
  - BusinessCloud, DatingScout — Thursday app closure documentation
  - Badoo biometric class action ($40M settlement) — public court records
- **Collection date**: March 2026
- **Scoring formula**: `DarkPatternScore = average(8 metric scores) × 12.5`
  - Each of 8 metrics scored 1–10 using anchored rubric (see plan.md)
  - Score range: 0–100. Higher = more manipulative.
  - Metrics: Swipe Addiction, Paywall Manipulation, Notification Spam, Visibility Throttling, Subscription Trap, Data Harvesting, FOMO Mechanics, Fake Scarcity
- **Sample size**: 23 dating apps
- **Tier thresholds**: S ≥ 85, A ≥ 70, B ≥ 55, C ≥ 40, D < 40

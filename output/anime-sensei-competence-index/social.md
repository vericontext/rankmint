## LinkedIn (2200 chars max)

**Primary post:**

We ranked 30 anime teachers on actual pedagogical effectiveness. Not power level. Not how cool their training montage looked. Curriculum design, student survival rate, emotional intelligence, consistent presence, measurable outcome delta, and equitable attention across all students.

The results inverted almost everything I expected.

Koro-sensei (#1, 9.70): A character whose entire arc is explicitly about being the perfect teacher scores like one. 28 individualized lesson plans. 10/10 on every dimension except outcome delta, where he is capped because his students start from civilian baseline. This is what it looks like when the writing takes pedagogy seriously.

Iruka Umino (#9, 7.62): A chunin Academy teacher with no famous battles outranks Gojo Satoru, All Might, and Kakashi combined. 10/10 availability. 10/10 student survival. 9/10 emotional intelligence. He is the first adult in Naruto who treats Naruto like a child. That is measurable. That is pedagogy.

Gojo Satoru (#26, 3.20): No documented curriculum. Imprisoned for a substantial portion of his students' development window. Extreme favoritism toward one student. His students improve largely through combat experience and their own talent. The formula is not fooled by the Six Eyes.

The formula: CompetenceScore = (0.20 x Curriculum) + (0.20 x Student Survival) + (0.18 x Emotional Intelligence) + (0.17 x Availability) + (0.15 x Outcome Delta) + (0.10 x Equitable Attention).

The two highest-weighted dimensions — curriculum coherence and survival rate — are exactly where the flashiest characters fail. All Might's curriculum is "here is a beach and a dream." Shanks met Luffy once. Kamina died in episode 8 and left his one student in psychological freefall.

## Reddit (r/anime, r/HunterXHunter, r/Naruto post body)

**Title:** I scored 30 anime teachers on actual teaching effectiveness (not power level) and made an interactive index. Results are controversial.

**Body:**

The premise: forget power levels. Forget how impressive the training arc looked. Score each teacher on what educational researchers actually measure:

- Curriculum coherence (did you have a plan?)
- Student survival rate (did your students survive what you trained them for?)
- Emotional intelligence (did you notice your students had feelings?)
- Availability and presence (were you actually there?)
- Outcome delta (how much did your students improve from baseline?)
- Equitable attention (did you invest in all your students, or just the favorite?)

**Top 5:**
1. Koro-sensei (9.70) — the formula confirms what the anime spent 22 volumes arguing
2. Biscuit Krueger (8.74) — structured Nen curriculum, equal Gon/Killua investment
3. Izumi Curtis (8.69) — highest outcome delta in the dataset
4. Wing (8.57) — safety-first Nen intro with real phase progression
5. Coach Anzai (8.15) — highest emotional intelligence among multi-student coaches

**Bottom 5:**
26. Gojo Satoru (3.20) — no curriculum, imprisoned half the series, extreme favoritism
27. Shanks (2.94) — one conversation; Luffy built everything himself
28. Endeavor (2.57) — what he did to Shoto was abuse, not training
29. Ging Freecss (2.35) — the entire HxH arc is Gon searching for his absent father
30. Kamina (2.06) — died in episode 8; left his one student in psychological freefall

**The controversy:** Might Guy (#7) outranks Kakashi (#20) by 2.4 points. Iruka Umino (#9) outranks both. Takeda Ittetsu (the Haikyuu faculty advisor who can't play volleyball) outranks Gojo by almost 4 points.

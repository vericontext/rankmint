## Email Body

**[HOOK]**

Here is the premise: what if we scored anime teachers the way educational researchers score real teachers?

Not power level. Not how impressive the training montage was. Not whether the student became the most powerful person in the series. Instead: curriculum design, consistent presence, emotional intelligence, equitable investment across all students, student survival rate, and measurable outcome delta.

We applied that formula to 30 teachers across shonen, seinen, sports, and mecha. The results inverted almost everything anime fandom assumes about mentorship.

---

**[KEY FINDINGS]**

**#1: Koro-sensei — 9.70**
The formula confirms what Assassination Classroom spent 22 volumes arguing: consistent, individualized, emotionally intelligent teaching is the hardest and most valuable work a teacher can do. Koro-sensei maintained individual files on all 28 members of Class 3-E. He adjusted his teaching to each student's psychological state. He produced a 100% student survival rate. He is the platonic ideal. The index says so.

**#7: Might Guy — 7.87**
The character coded as comic relief turns out to be the better teacher. Zero student deaths. 8/10 emotional intelligence (the Rock Lee recovery arc is one of the most attentive teacher moments in the Naruto series). 9/10 availability. His rival Kakashi? 5.47. Four tiers lower. The "joke character" was doing the real work.

**#9: Iruka Umino — 7.62**
No Sharingan. No sage mode. No One For All. A chunin Academy teacher with no famous battles, outranking Gojo Satoru (3.20), All Might (4.23), and Kakashi (5.47). The formula rewards what Iruka actually does: 10/10 availability (present for every lesson), 10/10 student survival (no Academy student died under his instruction), 9/10 emotional intelligence (he is the first adult in Naruto who treats Naruto like a child). That is measurable. That is pedagogy.

**#26: Gojo Satoru — 3.20**
The fan favorite. The most powerful jujutsu sorcerer in history. 2/10 curriculum (no documented lesson plans — experience teaches), 3/10 availability (imprisoned for a substantial arc), 2/10 equitable attention (extreme Yuji favoritism). His students improve largely through combat experience and their own talent. Gojo is not the teacher. He is the ceiling his students are trying to reach. That is not the same thing.

**#30: Kamina — 2.06**
The most inspiring mentor in the dataset. The worst teacher. One student, no curriculum, died in episode 8, left Simon in psychological freefall. "Believe in me who believes in you" is philosophy, not pedagogy. The index knows the difference.

**[THE FORMULA]**

CompetenceScore = (0.20 x CurriculumCoherence) + (0.20 x StudentSurvivalRate) + (0.18 x EmotionalIntelligence) + (0.17 x AvailabilityPresence) + (0.15 x StudentOutcomeDelta) + (0.10 x EquitableAttention)

The two highest-weighted dimensions — curriculum coherence and availability — are precisely where the most powerful characters fail. All Might's curriculum for Deku is: receive this power, here is a beach, I believe in you. Shanks met Luffy once. Kamina died in episode 8. The formula is not impressed by inspiration alone.

The Surprising Findings

Might Guy (#7) outranks Kakashi (#20) — by 2.4 points

The joke character turns out to have been doing the real work. Guy scores 10/10 on student survival, 8/10 on emotional intelligence (the Rock Lee recovery arc is one of the most attentive teacher moments in Naruto), and 9/10 on availability. Kakashi: 3/10 on equitable attention. The formula is not impressed by a Sharingan.

Iruka Umino (#9) outranks Gojo, All Might, and Kakashi — combined average

Iruka's 7.62 beats the combined average of Gojo (3.20), All Might (4.23), and Kakashi (5.47) — which is 4.30. He has no Sharingan, no Six Eyes, no One For All. He has 10/10 availability, 10/10 student survival, and 9/10 emotional intelligence. He is the first adult in Naruto who treats Naruto like a child. That is measurable. That is pedagogy.

Gojo Satoru (3.20) scores below Takeda Ittetsu (6.94) — the Haikyuu faculty advisor who cannot play volleyball

Takeda's direct contribution to skill development is minimal (outcome delta: 2/10). He secures match schedules and finds coaches. But he scores 10/10 on availability, 10/10 on student survival, 9/10 on equitable attention. Gojo: imprisoned, no curriculum, extreme favoritism. Reliable presence for every student beats intermittent genius for one. That is the thesis of this entire index.

Methodology

Formula: CompetenceScore = (0.20 x CurriculumCoherence) + (0.20 x StudentSurvivalRate) + (0.18 x EmotionalIntelligence) + (0.17 x AvailabilityPresence) + (0.15 x StudentOutcomeDelta) + (0.10 x EquitableAttention)

All metrics scored 0-10 using a four-anchor rubric applied to canonical anime and manga source material. Scores reflect analyst judgment applied consistently to the defined rubric.

  • Collection date: March 2026
  • Sample size: 30 teachers across shonen, seinen, sports, and mecha genres
  • Note: This is a qualitative pop culture index. Individual scores may vary by +/-1 depending on interpretation of edge cases. Formula and rubric published in full in plan.md.

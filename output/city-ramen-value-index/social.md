## Reddit Post (r/ramen, r/dataisbeautiful, r/JapanTravel)

**Title**: I built a 5-factor ramen value index for 30 cities. Fukuoka beats Tokyo. Bangkok ranks higher than New York. Here's the methodology.

**Body**:

Spent time building a City Ramen Value Index (CRVI) that tries to answer a more complete question than "which city has the best ramen." The formula:

CRVI = (0.25 x Affordability) + (0.20 x Shop Density) + (0.20 x Quality Ceiling) + (0.20 x Style Variety) + (0.15 x Cultural Embeddedness)

Affordability = avg local monthly wage / (avg bowl price x 30). Tokyo's bowl prices have crept up; New York's are brutal. Cultural embeddedness = rubric-scored measure of whether ramen is civic identity or a trend import.

**Top 10 results:**
1. Fukuoka — 9.21 (perfect cultural depth score, tonkotsu birthplace, lower prices than Tokyo)
2. Tokyo — 8.91 (perfect density, perfect variety, undeniable quality)
3. Osaka — 8.39
4. Taipei — 8.15 (the surprise — Michelin-trained talent, 8 styles, affordable)
5. Sapporo — 7.76
6. Seoul — 7.22
7. Bangkok — 6.90 (highest affordability score: 9.41/10)
8. Hong Kong — 6.50
9. Shanghai — 6.29
10. Singapore — 5.38

New York #17. London #27. Stockholm #30.

Full interactive charts linked below — there's a radar comparison tool where you can pit any two cities head-to-head.

Methodology is transparent. Happy to discuss any of the component scores — qualitative ones especially (Cultural Embeddedness is the hardest to pin down).

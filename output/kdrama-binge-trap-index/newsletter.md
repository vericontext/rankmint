## Version B — Long (600 words)

**Subject:** We ranked 30 K-dramas by how dangerous they are for your sleep schedule. IMDb's #1 came in #29.

The premise of the K-Drama Binge Trap Index is simple: which shows are most structurally engineered to disable your self-control?

Not which shows are best. Which ones make stopping feel physically impossible.

To answer this, we built the **Binge Trap Score (BTS)** — a weighted formula across five metrics:

- **Cliffhanger Density (30%)** — what percentage of episodes end on a hard cut at maximum tension
- **Romance Tension Engine (25%)** — how many episodes pass before the central romantic pair is definitively together, weighted by emotional charge
- **Spoiler Pressure (20%)** — how ferociously social media saturation forced viewers to watch immediately to avoid being spoiled
- **Episode Friction (15%, inverted)** — total watch hours; shorter shows score higher because they are easier to devour in one sitting
- **Plot Twist Velocity (10%)** — how frequently major reversals, betrayals, or identity reveals recontextualize everything

We scored 30 dramas. Here is what the data found.

---

**The headline finding: romance beats survival horror.**

Crash Landing on You finishes #1 with a BTS of 7.60 — above Squid Game's 7.40. The reason is the Romance Tension Engine. CLOY scores a perfect 10: for 16 full episodes, two people are separated by a military border and the full weight of geopolitical impossibility, and every episode ends on an emotional precipice. Squid Game scores a 2 on the same metric — romance is essentially absent. Survival fear resolves each episode. Romantic longing accumulates.

**The structural marvel: Penthouse.**

Critics spent years calling Penthouse lowbrow. By the engineering standards of this index, it is the most precisely constructed addiction machine among all 30 dramas. It is the only show to score a perfect 10 on both Cliffhanger Density and Plot Twist Velocity simultaneously — meaning virtually every episode ends mid-action AND contains at least one identity-recontextualizing reveal. Its only limiting factor: 48.75 total hours across three seasons creates physical friction. Without that, it would challenge for #1.

**The prestige paradox: Reply 1988 and My Mister.**

IMDb's top two K-dramas — both rated 9.0 — finish #29 and #30. Reply 1988 has a Cliffhanger Density of 2, a Spoiler Pressure of 3, and an Episode Friction score of 1 (20 × 95 minutes = 31.7 hours). My Mister has no central romance, no cliffhangers, and minimal spoiler urgency. Both are extraordinary dramas. Neither is a trap. The scatter matrix of IMDb Rating vs. Binge Trap Score shows a correlation close to zero — quality and addictiveness are almost completely orthogonal.

**The small drama that rockets up the chart: D.P.**

D.P. is six episodes long. At 5 total hours, it scores a perfect 10 on Episode Friction — the easiest binge on the entire list. Despite having minimal romance (Romance Tension Engine: 1), it finishes at #12 overall. The formula says: small and dangerous is often more dangerous than large and terrifying.

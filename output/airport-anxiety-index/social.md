## LinkedIn Post (long-form)

**The world's busiest airport is not the world's most stressful one.**

We ranked 30 major global hubs across 7 operational metrics — delay rates, security wait times, terminal congestion, wayfinding complexity, Wi-Fi quality, amenity quality, and immigration queues — and composite-scored each airport on a 0–100 anxiety scale.

The findings challenge almost every assumption travelers carry:

**Delhi (DEL) leads at 81.4 points.** The combination of 600,000 passengers per gate, 25-minute average security waits, and Wi-Fi speeds that average around 35 Mbps produces a score with no equivalent in the dataset. The gap between Delhi (#1, 81.4) and Sao Paulo Guarulhos (#2, 70.6) is larger than the gap between any other consecutive pair in the ranking.

**Paris CDG is ranked #6 in the world by Skytrax. It ranks #10 in our index.** The reason is structural: Terminal 2 alone has nine sub-terminals (2A through 2M). The average gate walk is documented at approximately one kilometer — more than double the industry standard. CDG's wayfinding normalization score is 100.0 — the worst of any airport we measured. Luxury lounges and award-winning food halls do not offset a structural labyrinth that has confused seasoned travelers for decades.

**Atlanta Hartsfield-Jackson (ATL) handles 108 million passengers.** It ranks 12th. The underground Plane Train connecting seven concourses, standardized signage, and Delta hub efficiency create a system that functions better under volume than airports half its size. The data decouples busyness from chaos.

**Mexico City (MEX) is routinely avoided.** Cirium ranked it 3rd globally for on-time performance in 2024, at 84.04% OTP. In our composite score, MEX finishes 18th — ahead of Frankfurt, Seoul Incheon, and Sydney. The reputation is wrong.

**Doha Hamad (DOH) is the calmest airport measured: 12.3 points.** The Skytrax #1 ranking is validated here, where it matters most — at the operational level, not the lounge level.

Data sources: US BTS, Cirium OTP 2024, Eurocontrol Annual Report, Ookla Q1 2024 airport Wi-Fi rankings, US CBP Airport Wait Times, Skytrax World Airport Awards 2024.
